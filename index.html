About

I like the breadth of general engineering — CAD one week, a thermal model the next.

Third-year MEng General Engineering student at the University of Sheffield, specialising in mechanical and on track for First-Class Honours (76% second-year average; Thermal & Fluids 87%, Materials 81%, Electromechanical 79%). My work runs from Energy-from-Waste facilities to biomimetic AUVs, and I take independent projects as far as physical prototypes.

Looking for a summer 2027 internship where I can contribute to real hardware.

Pocket-sized inflatable helmet

INDEPENDENT R&D · PROTOTYPING · MATERIALS
Problem
Cyclists skip helmets because they are awkward to carry. Could one inflate to full size and pack down to pocket size?
Approach
Self-directed: researched collapsing structures and the market to set the requirements, then fabricated prototypes in TPU-laminated nylon, testing thermal bonding, airtight sealing and pressure retention.
Outcome
Successive working prototypes, each narrowing down the bonding method that best held pressure.
