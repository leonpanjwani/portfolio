AUV

Biomimetic AUV — design & build

MECHATRONICS · TEAM PROJECT · FUSION 360
Problem
Build a working submersible that propels itself the way aquatic animals do, rather than with a conventional propeller.
Approach
Co-designed and constructed the vehicle, then used computational analysis to validate the propulsion and structure before manufacture.

Inflatable Helmet

Pocket-sized inflatable helmet

INDEPENDENT R&D · PROTOTYPING · MATERIALS
Problem
Cyclists skip helmets because they are awkward to carry. Could one inflate to full size and pack down to pocket size?
Approach
Self-directed: researched collapsing structures and the market to set the requirements, then fabricated prototypes in TPU-laminated nylon, testing thermal bonding, airtight sealing and pressure retention.
Outcome
Successive working prototypes, each narrowing down the bonding method that best held pressure.
